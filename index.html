TrailLink

Adaptive TinyML-Driven LoRa Emergency Beacon for Search and Rescue

Status: In Progress

TrailLink is my Final Year Project, which proposes a LoRa peer-to-peer (P2P) emergency beacon system for hikers in areas with limited or no cellular network coverage. The system is designed to transmit SOS messages and GPS coordinates to a receiver without relying on the Internet. It also explores TinyML-based adaptive transmission to improve communication reliability while managing battery consumption.

Technologies: LoRa P2P, TinyML, Arduino MKR WAN 1310, GPS, C/C++, Python
